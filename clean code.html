Executive & Managerial Hiring

Redefining Executive Search Through Trust & Strategic Insight

Global Executive Solutions Group (GESG) was established in 1995 and was originally incorporated as Integrated Executive Selection Corporation—also known as IXL Corporation. In 2004, the company was rebranded as Global Executive Solutions Group. Known simply as Global Executive or GESG, we have earned the lasting trust of major multinational corporations and domestic industry market leaders.

Why Choose GESG?

The executive search firm was established by professionals in their respective fields of expertise. It is one of the preferred headhunters in FMCG, Pharmaceutical, Power & Utilities, Retail, Real Estate, Fintech, and IT industries. We are also a valued partner of major conglomerates and selected banks.

Our Directors and Consultants are Development Dimensions International (DDI) trained in the Targeted Selection (TS) method.

Ray Silvestre Canilao

President & CEO
20+ Yrs Search | 30+ Yrs Marketing

Mr. Canilao is the President and CEO of Global Executive Solutions Group with more than twenty years of experience in Executive Search Management handling top-level search requirements, alongside thirty years of professional experience in Marketing and General Management.

He is a recipient of the prestigious 18th Agora Award for Outstanding Achievement in Marketing Management (National Level) for co-founding W.Brown and Co. Inc. in 1989.

Earlier in his marketing career, he managed major global brands such as Wrigley’s Juicy Fruit Gum, Bic ballpens, Duracell batteries, and Nike shoes.

Francisco C. Sebastian

Chairman
Chairman, GT Capital | Vice Chairman, Metrobank

Francisco C. Sebastian was re-elected as Chairman of GT Capital Holdings, Inc. in May 2022. Prior to assuming this post, he served as co-Vice Chairman of GT Capital.
